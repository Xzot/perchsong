Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

In short: Perch & Song collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Your region, collection and quiz results stay on your device.

1. What stays on your device

Your region, season setting, the birds you've heard, your quiz results and your streak are stored on your iPhone, in storage shared only between the app and its widgets. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

2. Location

If you tap Use my location once, iOS asks for permission. With your consent, the app reads one approximate location, works out which of six US regions you're in and saves only that region. The location itself is never stored and never leaves your device. You can pick a region by hand instead.

3. Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We receive no payment details. The app asks Apple which packs you own so it can unlock them.

4. No third parties

The app has no third-party SDKs, analytics or advertising. All birds, songs and paintings are bundled in the app, so it makes no network requests of its own apart from Apple's App Store services.

5. Children

Perch & Song is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: shchukavlad4@gmail.com.
