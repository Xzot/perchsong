Questions

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget. Find Perch & Song and pick Bird of the Day (small or medium) or Whose Song? (medium). The small size also shows in StandBy. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize and add Perch & Song under the clock.

The Play button doesn't make a sound

Check the volume and that another app isn't using the audio. The song plays even with the Silent switch on. The first tap after a restart can take a couple of seconds while iOS starts the app in the background. On the Lock Screen, iOS may ask you to unlock first.

How is the bird of the day chosen?

From the birds you have unlocked that typically live in your region in the current season. Pick your region in Settings (or use your location once). The season follows the calendar unless you choose one.

How does the quiz work?

Listen to a song and pick one of three birds. Get a bird right three times and it counts as known by ear. Birds you miss come back more often. The Whose Song? widget asks one question at a time, right on your Home Screen.

Is there a subscription?

No. Backyard Starter (20 birds) is free. Backyard Birds, Songbirds, Owls & Night Birds and Waterbirds are one-time purchases, and All Packs unlocks everything, including packs added later. Purchases work with Family Sharing.

How do I restore purchases?

Open Settings, then Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. It's on the purchase screen too.

Where do the songs and paintings come from?

Recordings are shared under open licenses (public domain, CC0 or CC BY) by iNaturalist observers and US agencies (National Park Service, Fish and Wildlife Service, Geological Survey). The paintings are public-domain plates from natural-history books published before 1931. Every recording and painting is credited in the app under Settings, Sound and illustration credits. Perch & Song is not affiliated with any of these organizations.
